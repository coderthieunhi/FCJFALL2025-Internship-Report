# Summary Report: AWS Cloud Day Vietnam 2025 - AI Edition

### Event Objectives

The primary goal of this event was to gather Vietnam's business leaders and technical builders to foster innovation in cloud computing and AI, contributing to the nation's digital transformation journey. Specifically, the event aimed to:
- Provide a platform for learning about the latest in generative AI and modern cloud solutions.
- Showcase real-world success stories and best practices to guide organizations in their adoption strategies.
- Strengthen the local ecosystem by connecting AWS experts, partners, startups, and attendees.

### Speakers

The event featured a lineup of distinguished speakers, including:
- **Eric Yeo** (Country General Manager, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos & Myanmar, AWS)
- **Dr. Jens Lottner** (CEO, Techcombank)
- **Ms. Trang Phung** (CEO & Co-Founder, U2U Network)
- **Jaime Valles** (VP, General Manager APJ, AWS)
- Along with technical experts from AWS such as **Jun Kai Loke**, **Kien Nguyen**, **Tamelly Lim**, **Binh Tran**, and **Taiki Dang**.

### Key Takeaways:

- Clear value for both business leaders and technical builders, with relevant tracks for each role.
- Real Vietnamese customer stories helped translate cloud/AI theory into practical adoption paths.
- Balanced program flow: strategy-focused keynotes followed by deep-dive breakouts for hands-on learning.
- Strong focus areas: Data & GenAI (analytics, GenAI apps) and Migration & Modernization (cloud moves, legacy upgrades, GenAI in DevOps).
- Direct access to AWS experts and partners created meaningful networking and collaboration opportunities.
- Live demos connected architectural concepts with concrete implementations and best practices.
- For students, a timely view of how cloud and AI are being applied in Vietnamese enterprises today.
- Overall, a structured experience that combined strategic vision with actionable techniques to build, scale, and modernize on AWS.
