### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
I truly appreciate the open and welcoming atmosphere at FCJ. The team fosters a sense of belonging, making it easy for newcomers to integrate and feel comfortable sharing ideas or asking for help. The workspace is well-organized and encourages productivity, while the supportive culture motivates me to keep learning and growing.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentorship here stands out for its patience and encouragement. My mentor not only guided me through technical challenges but also inspired me to think independently and explore solutions on my own. The admin team is proactive and attentive, ensuring all logistics and resources are available so I can focus on my work.

**3. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The internship provided highly relevant cloud knowledge and hands-on experience with AWS, which is invaluable for my future career. I gained practical skills in cloud architecture, teamwork, and professional communication. The exposure to production-ready projects and best practices has given me confidence to tackle new challenges in the tech industry.

**4. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
FCJ’s culture is built on respect, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Everyone is approachable and willing to share their expertise, which creates a dynamic learning environment. The team’s willingness to support each other, regardless of role, made me feel valued and motivated to contribute.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  
  > The most satisfying aspect was being part of an open and supportive community. The willingness of everyone to share knowledge, help each other, and collaborate made learning enjoyable and meaningful. I felt encouraged to ask questions, contribute ideas, and grow both technically and personally.

- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?  
  > I would definitely recommend this internship to a friend. It’s a great opportunity to gain relevant cloud knowledge and practical skills, while also experiencing a positive team culture. Interning here not only helps you improve yourself but also gives you a chance to contribute back to the community and be part of something bigger.

### Free Sharing

Looking back, I realize I could have been more proactive and dedicated throughout the internship. There were moments when I hesitated to take initiative or fully commit to tasks, and I see now how important these qualities are for both personal growth and team success. Despite this, the encouragement and support from the FCJ community inspired me to keep improving.

I genuinely hope to continue with the program, not only to further develop my own skills but also to contribute back to this amazing community. I want to share what I’ve learned, help others, and be part of the ongoing journey of growth and collaboration at FCJ and AWS Study Group community as a whole. Again. I much appreciate all the mentors for this opportunity.
